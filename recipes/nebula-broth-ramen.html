Shoyu Chicken Ramen

A rich and savory bowl of ramen featuring tender chicken in a deeply flavored soy-based broth, topped with soft-boiled eggs and green onions. An authentic Japanese comfort dish that's surprisingly achievable at home.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breasts or thighs
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup soy souce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 3 gloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 8oz ramen noodles
  • Nori (seaweed sheets), cut into strips

Preparation

Broth

  1. Bring chicken broth to a boil in a large pot
  2. Add chicken breasts or thighs whole
  3. Simmer for 20-25 minutes until cooked through
  4. Remove chicken and set aside to cool, then slice into bite-sized pieces
  5. In the same broth, add minced garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and sesame oil
  6. Simmer for 10 minutes to develop flavors

Eggs

  1. Bring a separate pot of water to a boil
  2. Gently add eggs and boil for 6-7 minutes for soft-boiled eggs
  3. Transfer to ice bath, then peel and halve

Noodles

  1. Cook ramen noodles according to package directions, usually 3-4 minutes
  2. Drain and set aside

Serving

  1. Divide cooked noodles among four bowls
  2. Pour hot broth over noodles
  3. Top with sliced chicken, soft-boiled egg halves, and green onions
  4. Garnish with nori strips
  5. Serve immediately while hot
  6. Enjoy!
