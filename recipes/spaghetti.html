Classic Spaghetti & Meatballs

Tender homemade meatballs simmered in a rich tomato sauce, served over al dente spaghetti for a comforting, timeless Italian-American favorite. Perfect for weeknight dinners and sure to please the whole family.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 minutes
  • Total Time: 45 minutes
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 2 gloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bottle marinara sauce
  • 1/2 tspn salt
  • 1/4 tspn black pepper
  • 6-8 cups water
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs

Preparation

Meatballs

  1. In a bowl, combine ground beef, breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, egg, minced garlic, salt & pepper
  2. Mix until just combined
  3. Roll mixture into 1-inch balls
  4. Heat oil into a large pan over medium-high heat
  5. Brown meatballs on all sides for 5-6 minutes
  6. Set aside on a plate

Marinara Sauce

  1. Pour Marinara sauce into the pan and heat for 2-3 minutes on medium-high heat
  2. Return meatballs into the pan
  3. Simmer meatballs on low heat for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed

Pasta

  1. Bring 6-8 cups of water to a boil on a large pot
  2. Add salt to water
  3. Add spaghetti and cook according to package instructions, usually 8-10 minutes
  4. Drain pasta in a colander and set aside

Serving

  1. Place cooked pasta on plates or bowls
  2. Top with meatballs & marinara sauce
  3. Garnish with extra Parmesan cheese
  4. Enjoy!
