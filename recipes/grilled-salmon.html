Grilled Salmon

Perfectly grilled salmon fillets with a crispy exterior and tender, flaky interior, seasoned simply with lemon and herbs. A healthy and elegant protein that's elegant enough for dinner guests yet easy enough for weeknight meals.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 12 minutes
  • Total Time: 22 minutes
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tspn dried dill
  • 1/2 tspn salt
  • 1/4 tspn black pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Preparation

Prep

  1. Pat thawed salmon fillets dry with paper towels
  2. Mix olive oil, minced garlic, dill, salt, and pepper in a small bowl
  3. Brush salmon fillets with the oil mixture on both sides

Grilling

  1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat
  2. Oil up grill grates to prevent sticking
  3. Place salmon skin-side up on grill
  4. Cook for 6-7 minutes without moving
  5. Flip carefully and cook skin-side down for another 5-6 minutes until sealed
  6. Salmon is done when it flakes easily with a fork

Serving

  1. Transfer salmon to plate
  2. Top with fresh lemon slices
  3. Garnish with fresh parsley
  4. Serve immediately while warm
  5. Enjoy!
