Grilled Bananas

Caramelized grilled bananas with a hint of cinnamon and brown sugar, topped with whipped cream and a drizzle of chocolate.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Cook Time: 10 minutes
  • Total Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 bananas
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup powdered cinammon
  • 1 tspn vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp chocolate sauce
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

Prep

  1. Peel bananas and slice them in half lengthwise
  2. Pat dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture

Grilling

  1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat
  2. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl
  3. Brush cut side of bananas with butter
  4. Sprinkle brown sugar and cinnamon mixture on buttered side
  5. Place bananas cut-side down on a grill, about 3-4 minutes until caramelized
  6. Flip and grill another 2-3 minutes on the skin side

Serving

  1. Transfer grilled bananas to plates
  2. Top with whipped cream
  3. Drizzle with chocolate sauce
  4. Serve immediately while warm
  5. Enjoy!
